# 《烬契圣堂》项目视觉参考图

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: project-level in-game visual reference, a real gameplay screenshot for a browser top-down action roguelike

Primary request: Create one polished, production-quality gameplay screenshot of Stage 3, the Broken-Oath Gallery, during a coherent live combat moment. It must look directly playable, never like a poster, concept-art board, mood board, splash illustration, or sprite sheet.

Camera and composition:
- Strict 90-degree top-down gameplay camera, landscape 16:9, entire rectangular combat room visible within the screen.
- Pixel-perfect game screenshot composition with a restrained HUD over the playfield.
- The room has broad circulation lanes and no dead ends. Cold green-gray cathedral masonry, charcoal voids, worn dark-red cloth, cracked flagstones, narrow wall alcoves, and sparse extinguished candle fixtures.
- Use two chunky stone pillars and several broken parallel pew segments as solid gameplay obstacles. Their silhouettes and collision edges must be crisp and immediately readable; they visibly block movement and projectiles.

Gameplay moment:
- Show exactly one player character: the Ember Warden, a nimble lantern hunter in a worn ritual robe, with warm-gold ember light concentrated only in the handheld lantern and sacred firearm. Strong compact silhouette, readable facing and aim direction.
- The Ember Warden is moving normally while freely aiming and firing one small straight warm-gold ember bolt toward an enemy. Show a clear thin aim direction and a small warm-gold crosshair on the floor, not a laser beam.
- Show exactly four normal enemies: exactly two Shadowfang Beasts and exactly two Shadow-Candle Attendants. No other characters.
- Shadowfang Beasts are low crouched quadrupedal shadow predators. One is closing distance; the other is visibly tightening its body before a short-range attack, with a localized red-violet warning wedge clearly separate from its dark body.
- Shadow-Candle Attendants are upright mid-range servants whose faces are hidden by extinguished candle crowns. One is aiming and releasing a single straight red-violet shadow dart; the other is repositioning behind a pillar. Their silhouettes must differ clearly from the beasts.
- Show several enemy shadow projectiles approaching the player's medium close range. Around the player, show only a subtle incomplete warm-gold circular timing cue indicating the Purifying Flame Pulse is ready and about to be used to erase projectiles. This is an attack-skill anticipation cue, not a shield, bubble, aura, immunity effect, dodge effect, or movement effect.
- All projectiles, warnings, characters, and obstacle edges must be distinguishable at a glance. Warm gold and near-white mean player/fire/usable power. Red-violet is reserved only for imminent enemy damage warnings and enemy projectiles. Decorative light stays dim desaturated amber and must never resemble a danger telegraph.

HUD:
- Top-left: a compact continuous player HP bar with a small heart/flame icon and crisp pixel numerals `76 / 100`; beneath it, one compact Purifying Flame Pulse skill icon glowing warm gold to communicate READY, with no cooldown timer.
- Top-right: exactly two compact held-sigil icons, equal size, with simple distinct glyphs and no explanatory panels.
- Combat area: the small aim crosshair described above.
- No mob HP bars. Absolutely no boss, boss name, or boss HP bar. No minimap, inventory window, choice panel, dialogue, tutorial, watermark, logo, title, caption, or decorative border.

Style and rendering:
- High-contrast stylized Pixel Art made for an actual shipped game, crisp hard pixel clusters, deliberate limited palette, chunky readable silhouettes, dark selective outlines, no anti-aliased painterly brushwork, no 3D rendering.
- Dense enough for a modern 16:9 game screen while preserving authentic pixel-art clustering and clear gameplay readability at thumbnail size.
- Environment hierarchy: low-contrast cold green-gray stone and charcoal shadows; mid-contrast architecture and obstacles; brightest warm-gold player attacks and red-violet enemy danger.
- Lighting is restrained and local. Avoid bloom haze that softens collision boundaries.

Strict exclusions:
- No rolling pose, somersault, dash, teleport, speed trail, afterimage, dodge smear, invincibility frames, shield, protective dome, or full-body immunity aura.
- No Boss or elite enemy, no Boss HP, no extra enemies, no friendly companion, no loot-choice screen.
- No perspective/isometric/side view; no dramatic cinematic close-up; no poster composition.
- No readable prose, labels, speech bubbles, or long text. Only the HUD numerals `76 / 100` are allowed.
